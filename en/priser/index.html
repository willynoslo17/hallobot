What does it really cost?

Start-up is paid once. The monthly price covers operation, license for the chatbot, updates and report. No binding period.

Not included

  • Changes to your website itself (beyond adding the chatbot).
  • Integrations that are not included in the package.
  • Answers from a human in the chat (inquiries are sent to you).

Frequently asked questions

What do you need from me?

Content from the website and FAQ, and access to embed the code on the website (or help from your web developer).

Does it work with my website?

It works for most websites that allow you to enter one line of code. I will check it in the free call.

What if the chatbot answers incorrectly?

It is set up to respond based on the content you have approved and tell you when it doesn't know. If something goes wrong, I'll fix it in the monthly update.

How many calls are included?

Normal use for a small business is included. If you get a lot of calls, I'll tell you before anything changes.

Can I resign whenever I want?

Yes, there is no lock-in period.
