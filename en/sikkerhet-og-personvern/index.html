Security and privacy

This is how I think of privacy when the chatbot is on your website - without promises that depend on platform choice.

Who is responsible

You (the company) are responsible for processing the calls. I act as a data processor and sign one data processor agreement with each customer.

What data

The text of the conversations, and contact information that visitors themselves choose to leave.

Where the data is stored and subcontractors

Which subcontractors are used and where the data is stored is stated in the data processing agreement.

Transparency

The chatbot says it's an AI from the first message, and visitors can ask to speak to a human.

Deletion

The conversations are deleted according to what has been agreed in the data processing agreement.

What you should do

Mention the chatbot in your own privacy policy. I provide a model text that you can adapt.

Sensitive data

The chatbot must not ask for sensitive information (health, social security number, payment card).
