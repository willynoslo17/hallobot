This is how it works

From free calls to chat on your website - with training on your content and monthly follow-up.

The schedule is agreed in the offer.

The steps

  1. 1. Free call

    30 minutes, on video or in person in Oslo. I hear about your needs and whether the Hallobot fits.

  2. 2. Fixed offer with written scope

    You will receive the price and what is included in writing - before I start.

  3. 3. You send me the content

    The website, opening hours, prices, frequently asked questions and how you want to be contacted.

  4. 4. I train the chatbot

    I write and adapt the answers in Norwegian, Spanish and English based on the content you have approved.

  5. 5. You test and approve

    You try out the calls and let us know if anything needs to be changed before launch.

  6. 6. The chatbot is added to your website

    With one line of code. I will help you or your web developer.

  7. 7. Every month: update and report

    Update of the content and a short report (number of calls, FAQs and inquiries, as the platform shows them).
